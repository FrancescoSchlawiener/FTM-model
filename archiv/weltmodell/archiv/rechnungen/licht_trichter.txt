Einheiten-Kontrolle
==========================================================================================
  [3c^2 Ds]   = m^2 s^-2 * m        = m^3 s^-2 = [GM]
  [c_eff]     = m s^-1 * 1          = m s^-1
  [g]         = m^2 s^-2 * m / m^2  = m s^-2
  [delta]     = 1 ;  [g r / c^2] = m s^-2 * m / (m^2 s^-2) = 1
  [r*]        = sqrt(m^3 s^-2 / (m s^-2)) = m ;  [r*/r0] = 1
