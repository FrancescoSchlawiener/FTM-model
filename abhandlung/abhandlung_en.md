## Abstract

Space is described as a closed, folded three-dimensional manifold, pictured as a funnel. Mass is fold, Δs = GM/(3c²). Slope, clock rate and energy follow from it. Because space has no outside, every motion returns in an inner direction that closes at the closure length Λ. From this follow a threshold a₀ = c²/(2Λ) and a rule of motion, a = a_N/(1 − e^{−√(a_N/a₀)}), which is Newtonian above the threshold and gives flat rotation curves below it, without additional mass. The rotation curves of 141 galaxies (SPARC) read a₀ = 1.11·10⁻¹⁰ m/s², the scale of the early sound wave (DESI) 1.10, the shape of the distances of supernovae and BAO 1.09: the same threshold for part and whole. The breathing after Planck reads 1.065. Dark energy is the restoring of the three loops of the torus. From a₀ and the measured densities of the baryons and the radiation follow H₀ = 67.2 km/s/Mpc and Ω_DE = 0.685. All levels are ordered by three symmetries of the torus shape, mirror, threefold and full turn, and by two ladders. Nucleus, atom, substances, stars and galaxies show the same forms, tested on nuclear masses (AME2020), on the periodic table (PubChem) and on the galaxies.

## Preface: Terms Are Tools

This treatise describes the world with a few images: funnel, fold, slope, clock rate, return, loop, closure. These words are tools, not new things. Behind each of them stands a mathematical object that geometry named long ago. The most important one is the **manifold**: a space that looks like ordinary flat space in the small, but may be curved, folded and closed in on itself in the large. The surface of the Earth is a two-dimensional manifold: every piece looks flat like a sheet of paper, while the whole is a sphere without an edge.

**Fold** and **funnel** are, in this model, the same as a curved three-dimensional manifold, only named more vividly. We keep our terms because they help to explain: a funnel can be drawn, a metric cannot. Wherever a term has its own precise meaning in the model, it is explained where it first appears. All symbols are collected in Appendix A.

One rule holds throughout: nothing is explained as an additional substance. Everything observed is a state or a rule of the one medium, space itself.

**The course of the treatise.** Part I starts with the funnel: what the fold is, and how slope, clock rate and energy follow from it. Part II closes space: because it has no outside, every motion returns, and the threshold and the rule follow from that return. Part III shows the symmetries that carry the whole, the mirror, the threefold and the full turn, and the two ladders on which all levels are ordered. Part IV goes through the levels from the particle to the galaxy, Part V through the whole with threshold, breathing and dark energy. Part VI explains how to read measurements without mistaking rules for things, what can be tested and what remains open. Every step builds only on the previous ones.

# Part I: The Funnel

## 1 The Funnel as an Image of the Folded Manifold

We live in three spatial directions. We cannot look at a curvature of this three-dimensional space from outside, because there is no outside from which to look. One helps oneself with a cut: take a flat plane through the middle of a body, say the Sun, and draw how long the paths in this plane really are. Where space is folded, the paths inward are longer than flat geometry would lead us to expect. Drawing this extra length as depth pointing downward produces a **funnel**.

The funnel therefore shows the three-dimensional manifold in two dimensions. Its depth is not a place one could fall into, but a measure: a funnel that sinks deeper represents **more space within space**. Between two circles of equal circumference, the funnel holds a longer path than the flat plane. Space contains more there than its boundary reveals.

## 2 The Fold Δs

The **fold** Δs is the extra length in the centre of a body that folded space contains compared with flat space. It is the measure of mass:

```
Δs = GM/(3c²)
```

**Derivation.** The fold grows with the mass, and it is distributed over the body: for a body of mass M, volume V, surface S and radius R, the extra radial length is Δs = (GM/c²)·V/(S·R), the length GM/c² weighted by the ratio of content to boundary. In the language of general relativity this is the excess radius of a homogeneous sphere. The ratio V/(S·R) is a pure shape number: for the sphere, V = (4/3)πR³ and S = 4πR², so V/(S·R) = 1/3. Hence the 3 in the denominator: it is the spatial dimension d = 3. A homogeneous sphere in d dimensions has Δs = GM/(d·c²).

**Examples.** The Earth carries a fold of Δs = 1.478 mm, the Sun of Δs = 492.2 m, the Milky Way with 6·10¹⁰ solar masses of Δs = 197.4 AU. The fold depends only on the mass, not on the radius: a star that inflates keeps its fold.

## 3 The Slope g

The **slope** is the steepness of the funnel wall at a place, what we feel as weight:

```
g = GM/r² = 3c²·Δs/r²
```

It follows from the fold by inserting GM = 3c²Δs. The product g·r² = 3c²Δs is fixed: the fold sits in the centre, and the slope falls off outward with the square of the distance. If a body expands at fixed mass, its edge flattens while the fold in the centre remains (the balloon effect): a red giant is deeply folded and only weakly sloped at its surface.

## 4 The Clock Rate and Time

The **clock rate** dτ/dt is the ratio in which space unrolls at a place, compared with flat outer space. It follows from the fold, since the clock rate at distance r is √(1 − 2GM/(rc²)), and 2GM/c² = 6Δs:

```
dτ/dt = √(1 − 6Δs/r),        δ = 1 − dτ/dt ≈ 3Δs/r
```

**Time** is the difference in unrolling between folded and flat regions, not friction and not a flow of its own. It arises only where space is folded. Between two places it is δ_AB = dτ/dt|_B − dτ/dt|_A. The small quantity δ is called the **spread**: the distance of the clock rate from its flat value of one.

At the **limit radius** r₀ = 6Δs the clock stands still. For the Earth it lies at 8.87 mm, for the Sun at 2.95 km. A clock on the Earth's surface falls behind flat outer space by 0.022 s per year, on the Sun's surface by 67 s. In the Sun's funnel at the Earth's orbit the figure is 0.31 s. On a neutron star (1.4 M☉, 12 km) the clock rate is 0.81.

**Light in the clock rate.** c is the scale of the unfolded envelope: c² = GM/(3Δs) links fold and slope. Measured at the place, light is always c, because clock and light follow the same rate. Counted from outside it is slower, c_eff = c·√(1 − 6Δs/r), a delay of the unrolling, not friction.

## 5 Energy Is Bound Fold

Mass and energy are the same, and both are fold:

```
E = Mc² = 3c⁴·Δs/G
```

Every energy, whether rest, motion or field, is bound fold. When a body binds to another, it gives up fold.

**Derivation of binding.** On a circular orbit in the funnel, v² = GM/r = 3c²Δs/r, hence δ = 3Δs/r = v²/c²: the spread of an orbit is the square of its speed relative to c. The binding energy of a circular orbit is ½mv², that is ½·δ of the rest energy. **Binding gives up half the spread.**

**Examples.** In the hydrogen atom the electron circulates with v/c = α, the fine-structure constant. The binding is therefore α²/2 of the electron's rest energy, 2.661·10⁻⁵ including the co-motion of the nucleus. The measured 13.598 eV (PubChem) is exactly this fraction. The iron nucleus ⁵⁶Fe carries 0.94 % less mass than its nucleons would carry separately (AME2020).

## 6 Orbits and Light in the Funnel

A planet follows the slope that the Sun's fold produces at its orbital radius:

```
v = c·√(3Δs☉/r),        T² = 4π²·r³/(3c²·Δs☉)
```

With Δs☉ the fold of the Sun this gives for the Earth the orbital speed v = 29.78 km/s and the period T of one year. Every motion in the funnel feels two things: the slower clock rate and the extra length. For light both parts are equal:

```
deflection = 6Δs/b (clock rate) + 6Δs/b (fold) = 12Δs/b        solar limb: 0.8756″ + 0.8756″ = 1.7512″
```

Here b is the distance at which the ray passes the centre. Orbits feel both too: the perihelion of an orbit advances by (12π + 12π − 6π)·Δs/p = 18πΔs/p per revolution, with p the parameter of the orbit, its half width through the focus. For Mercury that is 42.98″ per century. Expressed with the spread δ = 3Δs/p: the orbit misses the full turn by 3δ, and light is deflected by 4δ.

The funnel has fixed **steps**: at r₀ = 6Δs the clock stands still, at 9Δs lies the photon sphere, at 18Δs the innermost stable orbit (Sun 4.43 and 8.86 km). The circles 6Δs, 9Δs, 18Δs are 2d, 3d, 6d with d = 3: the whole numbers of the funnel are multiples of the dimension.

# Part II: The Closed Space

## 7 No Outside: the Torus

Space has no edge and no outside. It loops back into itself. The simplest form of such a space in three directions is the **torus T³**: take a cube and declare opposite faces to be the same. Whoever walks out on the right comes back in on the left. Every direction therefore has a finite **loop**, a closed path that leads around space. The torus T³ has three independent loops.

Because there is no outside, fold cannot drain away anywhere. When funnels fold extra length into their centres, the closed medium answers with a stretching of the loops:

```
σ = ΣΔs_centres − ΔL_space = 0
```

The medium thus runs a cycle: flat matrix (Δs = 0), vortex (slope g), fold (extra length Δs in the centre), return through the closed whole. Charges, by contrast, cancel on the closed space, ΣQ_el = 0. And the form of the slope is constrained: the torus has Euler characteristic zero, so every slope obeys funnels − filament saddles + wall saddles − voids = 0 (Section 28).

## 8 The Inner Direction and the Return

Because space is closed, every motion returns. This **return** does not run in space, but in an **inner direction** ϖ, orthogonal to space: a half-line from the place, closed at the **closure length** Λ. It is not a spatial direction. One cannot travel in it. It is the path on which the state of a place finds its way back to itself.

The return obeys a single equation, a random walk with loss, for the share u of the state that is at depth ϖ at time t:

```
∂_t u + (Γ + θ)·u = D·∂_ϖ²u,     Γ = a_N/c,     D = GM/c
```

Here a_N = GM/r² is the Newtonian slope at the place, Γ the loss from the clock-rate gradient, θ the loss from the expansion of the region, and D the spreading in the inner direction.

**Derivation of the return fraction.** Stationary and without expansion, for the profile u = G(ϖ) the equation D·G″ = Γ·G remains, with G(0) = 1 (G here this profile, not the gravitational constant). The solution falls off exponentially, and at the return depth ϖ_R

```
q = G(ϖ_R) = e^{−ϖ_R·√(Γ/D)} = e^{−s},     s² = Γ·τ_D,     τ_D = ϖ_R²/D
```

q is the **return fraction**: the share of the state that comes back to the place.

**The state space.** A point of the state space is a complete state of a system, not a place and not a particle. Every independent direction of circulation is a degree of freedom, and n circulations span a torus Tⁿ. Every force acts in it in one of three roles: **tangential** (it conserves energy and entropy and produces circulations and phases, like the electromagnetic one), **transverse** (it produces entropy and pulls onto the torus, like heat and radiation) or **parametric** (it slowly shifts the torus itself). In one equation: ż = 𝓛(z)∇E + 𝓜(z)∇S + ε·F_par, with 𝓛 = −𝓛ᵀ and 𝓛∇S = 0 (tangential), 𝓜 = 𝓜ᵀ ≥ 0 and 𝓜∇E = 0 (transverse). Gravity is parametric: it is not a direction of circulation, but fixes which orbits exist and which degrees of freedom are independent. It acts through two channels, the pre-tension (it changes the stiffness and with it which circulations exist) and the clock rate (it shifts all frequencies alike). A slow change keeps the action J = E/ω fixed, the torus moves to the neighbouring fibre of equal action. Every inner coupling term appears twice in the energy balance with opposite signs, it only shifts energy. Only gravity and externally driven parameters can supply it. A tangential coupling alone can never lock in. Locking needs loss.

**Derivation of the threshold.** An exactly open direction would have an infinite mean return time. The inner direction is closed at Λ. By Kac's theorem the mean return time to the place is then the path there and back, τ_D = 2Λ/c, the same for every mass. With Γ = a_N/c we get s² = a_N·τ_D/c, and with the **threshold**

```
a₀ = c/τ_D = c²/(2Λ)
```

it follows that s² = a_N/a₀. Kac's theorem holds for every closed state space: for every partition into regions, Σᵢ 1/⟨T_{Aᵢ}⟩ = 1, the mean return time into a region being the total measure divided by its measure. Closure thus distributes a fixed budget of recurrence. An open space sets it to zero. For the dwell times of a closed, stationary system between two states, Σ 1/⟨τ⟩ = 1: a direct test of closedness. If the region expands, the loss θ from the expansion is added. Whether the rule acts on a perturbation is then decided by the sum s² = a_N/a₀ + θ̃·τ_D, not by a_N/a₀ alone, with θ̃ the expansion rate of the region, the loss θ of the return equation. The model works with a₀ = 1.06·10⁻¹⁰ m/s². This belongs to Λ = 13.7 Gpc and τ_D = 89.6 billion years. τ_D is longer than the age of the universe: the return is not a process along the orbit, but a stationary loop at a fixed place. Its depth follows from τ_D = ϖ_R²/D with D = GM/c: ϖ_R² = 2GMΛ/c² = r₀·Λ, and that is r*² (Section 10). The return depth is the threshold radius.

## 9 The Rule

Counted at the place, all returns add up: the state comes back with the fraction q, of that again q, and so on. The renewal u(0) = a_N + u(ϖ_R), each return an event in one sense of circulation, gives the **rule**:

```
a = ν·a_N,     ν = Σ qⁿ = 1/(1 − q) = 1 + n_B(s),     n_B = 1/(e^s − 1),     q = e^{−√(a_N/a₀)}
```

ν is called the **enhancement**. It has the form of one plus a Bose occupation. What is counted are returns, not particles. In general a feedback loop of gain q sums all returns to Σqⁿ = 1/(1 − q): q < 0 damps, 0 < q < 1 enhances, q ≥ 1 runs away. The sign belongs to the whole closure, not to the single link.

**The two limits.** If the slope is large compared with the threshold (a_N ≫ a₀), s is large, q → 0, ν → 1: Newton holds exactly. If it is small (a_N ≪ a₀), ν ≈ 1/s, so

```
a → √(a_N·a₀) = √(GM·a₀)/r,     v² = a·r = √(GM·a₀),     v⁴ = GM·a₀
```

The orbital speed becomes independent of the radius: this is the **flat rotation curve** of galaxies, and v⁴ ∝ M is the Tully–Fisher relation. What the observer reads as dark matter is this changed rule of motion, not additional mass. The rule has exactly one branch: d ln a/d ln a_N = 1 − χ/2 lies strictly between ½ and 1, without jump and without hysteresis, with the switch χ = s/(e^s − 1) (Section 15), and d ln a/d ln a₀ = χ/2. The deep regime is scale-invariant (r → λr, t → λt, a → a/λ). From this follows v⁴ ∝ M.

The transition lies at the **threshold radius**, where a_N = a₀:

```
r* = √(GM/a₀)
```

There ν = 1.582. **Example:** the Milky Way as a point mass of 6·10¹⁰ solar masses has r* = 8.88 kpc. The rule gives v = 249 km/s at 5 kpc, 214 at r*, 196 at 15 kpc, 178 at 50 kpc, against the deep value of 170.4 km/s. The Sun has its threshold far outside, at r* = 7480 AU. The planetary system lies deep in the Newtonian regime.

**The balance.** The rule is a balance like that between emission and absorption of light: emission ∝ 1 + n_B, absorption ∝ n_B, and their ratio is q. Newton is the spontaneous part, the return the stimulated one. Decomposed, ν = ½ + ½·coth(s/2): a fixed half and the fluctuating part of an oscillator.

**Light and orbits.** The rule acts at the place of the orbit on all matter. The metric carries only its curl-free part, ν·g_N = −∇Φ + ∇×A: light follows the metric and sees only −∇Φ, while orbits also see the vortex ∇×A. The slope that light follows obeys ∇²Φ = ∇·[ν·∇Φ_N], and both potentials of the metric are equal, Ψ = Φ. For spheres the vortex vanishes, and there orbits and light read the same mass. In discs the dynamical mass inside is larger than the lensing mass. Far outside r* the deflection of light tends to the fixed value α_∞ = 2π·(v_flat/c)², for the Milky Way 0.42″. Conversely, the flat rotation reads the fold directly, Δs = v_flat⁴/(3c²a₀). Except for spheres, the rule has no action in the sense of an energy: its force −ν∇Φ_N is a gradient only when ν is constant on the Newtonian equipotential surfaces. It acts on the generator of motion (Section 29), not on the energy. On stellar orbits the exchange of work averages out, and the field form ∇·[ν∇Φ_N] does not enter the rotation curves: the rule acts at the place of the orbit. In the solar system the return vanishes to every order. At the Earth's orbit the addition is e^{−7480}: the known tests of gravity remain untouched (γ = β = 1), and gravitational waves travel at c, with two polarisations transverse to the direction, from the changing quadrupole of the fold. Their sources always lie far above the threshold frequency Ω(r*)/π, so they are single funnels. In the strong field the return is without effect, waveform and ringdown are those of general relativity. The rise of the frequency, ḟ = (96/5)·π^{8/3}·(3Δs_c/c)^{5/3}·f^{11/3} with Δs_c the fold of the chirp mass of the pair, reads the fold of the source, just as the flat rotation reads that of a galaxy. The breathing is global and does not radiate.

## 10 The Triad and the Three Radii

Every field on the closed space splits into three parts, the **triad**: the **background** (harmonic, without sources, the loops), the **vortex** (rotation and remaining slope, the return at the place) and the **centre** (the flux out of the mass). The field of the parent level is background for the child level. In this way the triad repeats on every level.

Every body has, in addition, three radii:

```
r₀ = 6Δs (centre),     R (envelope, vortex),     r* = √(GM/a₀) = c·√(3Δs/a₀) (transition to the background)
```

The envelope R is the surface of the body. Between r₀ and R runs the vortex, outside r* the background carries. At the threshold radius the spread is the square of the flat rotation speed, δ(r*) = 3Δs/r* = (v_flat/c)². Compact bodies have their threshold far outside their envelope and remain Newtonian. Galaxies have it within their extent. Atom, star, galaxy and cell are the same funnel. They differ only in two ratios: fold to envelope (Δs/R: clock rate and edge) and envelope to threshold (R/r*: Newton or return). Dark matter is the second ratio, not a substance. The whole lies at its own threshold with r*/Λ just below one.

# Part III: Symmetries and Ladders

## 11 The Three Generators

The torus has not only loops, it has a **shape**: a flat torus can be square, rectangular or skewed. This shape is described by a single complex number, the **modulus** τ. Different values of τ can describe the same torus. The conversions between them are the symmetries of the torus shape. They form a group, PSL(2,ℤ), and this group has exactly two **generators** from which every other symmetry is composed:

```
mirror               τ → −1/τ          applied twice: the identity
threefold rotation   τ → −1/(τ + 1)    applied three times: the identity
                     PSL(2,ℤ) = ℤ₂ ∗ ℤ₃
```

The sign ∗ is the free product: every symmetry is a chain of the two generators. To these belongs the **full turn** τ → τ + 1: once all the way around, the closure per turn.

Their fixed points are the two distinguished shapes: the square torus (90°, fixed point of the mirror) and the hexagonal one (60°, 120°, fixed point of the threefold rotation). Only these rotations carry the shape of the torus into itself. This is why light that crosses the cell, the cube of the torus (Section 7), several times can only arrive rotated by 60°, 90°, 120° and 180°. The cell carries only the modes k = 2π·n/L. At the best point of all precision data (Q = 2π − 1, maximum of the dark energy at saturation) L is 0.998 times the diameter of the last scattering surface, and the cube lowers the quadrupole of the background radiation by 13 % and raises the octupole by 2 %. On Planck's data below ℓ = 30 this changes χ² by only 0.08, so the cell cannot be read yet.

Every symmetry of the shape is, by its **trace**, of one of three kinds:

| Trace | Fixed point | Kind | In the model |
|---|---|---|---|
| below 2 | inside | mirror, threefold rotation | **meeting points** |
| equal to 2 | one, on the boundary, rational | full turn | **closure**, resonance |
| above 2 | two, on the boundary, irrational | passing through, simplest: fixed point at the golden ratio | **open winding** |

Meeting point, closure and open winding are the three kinds of one and the same group. Orbits run on tori with irrational winding, and no loop ever closes exactly. The golden winding is the most stable under perturbation, because it lies furthest from every rational resonance. Mirror, threefold and full turn, which recur on every level of the model, are therefore not separate findings, but the whole symmetry of the torus shape.

Every phenomenon is thus the return of a closed path, counted at a place, weighted with a nome q = e^{2πiτ}. Its modulus |q| is the loss, with one sense of circulation: gravity, rule of return, weight of the closure rule, vacuum part. Its phase is the winding, lossless, in both senses of circulation: charge, spin, orbit, entanglement.

## 12 The Mirror on Every Level

A **mirror** is a fixed product of two quantities: if one grows, the other shrinks in the same ratio, and both lie symmetrically about their geometric mean. The basic case is the fold itself. Every mass has two lengths: the fold 3Δs = GM/c², which grows with the mass, and the **quantum length** λ̄ = ħ/(Mc), which falls with the mass. Their product does not depend on the mass:

```
λ̄·3Δs = (ħ/Mc)·(GM/c²) = ħG/c³ = ℓ_P²
```

The **Planck length** ℓ_P is the centre of the mirror. Both lengths meet at the Planck mass m_Pl. The coupling of gravity, α_G = (M/m_Pl)², is the distance from the meeting point.

The same form recurs on every level. Fanned out:

**How each row arises.**

- *Star:* with 3Δs_* = GM_*/c², M_* = m_Pl³/m_p² and ℓ_P·m_Pl = ħ/c, one gets ℓ_P·3Δs_* = ħ²/(m_p²c²) = λ̄_p².
- *Atom:* the electron radius is r_e = α·λ̄_e, the Bohr radius a_B = λ̄_e/α. Their product is λ̄_e², and α drops out.
- *Charge:* by Dirac, the product of the electric charge e and the magnetic charge g is a whole multiple of the quantum of action. The meeting point of both is the Planck charge q_P = e/√α.
- *Heat:* a fold has the temperature kT = ħc/(24πΔs) (k the Boltzmann constant), its thermal length is λ_th = ħc/kT = 24πΔs. Multiplied by λ̄ = ħ/(Mc) this gives 8π·ħG/c³ = 8πℓ_P².
- *Rotation:* a rotating funnel has two horizons r± = GM/c² ± √((GM/c²)² − a_J²) with the twist a_J = J/(Mc), J the angular momentum. Their product is r₊·r₋ = a_J², their sum 6Δs. At spin number j = a_J/(3Δs) = 1 both coincide. In the equatorial plane the clock rate of resting clocks remains exactly √(1 − 6Δs/r). Co- and counter-rotating orbits differ by 4π·a_J/c, independent of the radius, and the twist separates the vertical cycle of an orbit from the revolution: the orbital plane precesses, the orbital torus goes from T² to T³.
- *Funnel:* r*² = GM/a₀ = (2GM/c²)·(c²/(2a₀)) = r₀·Λ.
- *Return:* Section 16. *Whole and place:* Section 15. *Vacuum:* Section 25. *Part and whole:* Section 23.
- *Loops:* if one counts not only the returns of a loop but all its multiples, the sum is Z = ∏ 1/(1 − qⁿ), up to q^{1/24} the reciprocal of the η-function of the torus. Its mirror η(−1/τ) = √(−iτ)·η(τ) maps the Newtonian regime onto the deep one, s ↔ 4π²/s. In the deep regime ln Z = π²/(6s) + ½·ln(s/2π) − s/24, and the leading part is the first step of the ladder.
- *Forces:* a force r^a and a force r^A are mirror partners when (a + 3)(A + 3) = 4: the map u = z^{(a+3)/2} of the plane (z the place as a complex number) carries the orbits of one into the orbits of the other. With u = z², the ellipse about the centre, the orbit inside a uniformly held body (r¹), becomes the Kepler ellipse about the focus (r⁻²). The mirror point of bound orbits is the exponent −1: exactly the force of the deep regime, √(GMa₀)/r. The second, the exponent −5, leads to the fall into the centre. The rule runs from Newton to the meeting point of the force laws.

Together they show: the levels of the world are mirror images of one another, reflected about fixed centres, and the whole stands to the part in the same form as the proton to the Planck point.

## 13 Meeting Points and Spread

The fixed points of the mirror are the **meeting points**: the places where motion reaches c. There, centre, mirror and envelope coincide. At the Planck point this happens completely (3Δs = λ̄ = ℓ_P), and the funnel is degenerate. At the other end of the ladder the whole lies almost the same way, r₀/Λ = 6/(1 + Q), r*/Λ = √(6/(1 + Q)), with Q the weight of the mode of the whole (Section 24).

The **spread** of every level is the speed of its motion relative to c, the distance from the meeting point:

- For the atom it is α = v/c. Winding is phase, an angle, linear in the speed.
- For the funnel it is δ(r*) = 3Δs/r* = (v_flat/c)², the ratio of the radii r₀/r* = 2δ(r*). Fold is magnitude, a clock rate, quadratic like δ = v²/c².

Coupling constants are thus not arbitrary numbers, but distances from the meeting point of their level.

## 14 The Ladder of Lengths

Five lengths place every mass: the quantum length λ̄ (at the place), the Planck length ℓ_P, the fold 3Δs, the threshold radius r* (in space) and the **length of action** ℓ₀ = 2Λ (in the inner direction). They form a **ladder**. Where two lengths become equal lies a distinguished mass. Their positions hang on a single number, the ratio of the Planck length to the length of action:

These are not bodies, but mass scales at which two lengths become equal. Both ends are set by the return time τ_D, the lower one through ħ, the upper one through G. The function of the rule itself forms a ladder. Each step is the previous one times s:

## 16 The Return as a Mirror Image

If one reads the time of the return as imaginary time, the return equation becomes the Schrödinger equation in the inner direction ϖ, with D = ħ/(2m̃) and the slope as the potential, V = ħΓ = ħa_N/c.

**Derivation.** From D = GM/c and D = ħ/(2m̃) follows the **mirror mass**

```
m̃ = ħc/(2GM) = m_Pl²/(2M),        λ̄(m̃) = ħ/(m̃c) = 2GM/c² = r₀
```

The funnel M returns like the quantum motion of its mirror mass, and the quantum length of that mass is its limit radius. The return fraction q = e^{−s} is the tunnelling factor of this mirror image through the slope, the same form as e^{−√(E_G/E)} when two nuclei of energy E pass through their Coulomb barrier (E_G the Gamow energy). This is why the period of the return is a temperature, kT_I = ħ/τ_D = ħa₀/c, and why the rule has the Bose form: heat is periodicity in imaginary time. The geometric distribution of the number of returns has, at fixed mean, the largest entropy, H = ln ν + χ. The threshold itself compares two such quantities: the Unruh temperature T_U of the observer held in place against the slope, with the period of the inner direction, s² = 2π·T_U/T_I. This is a period, not heat at the place. Gravity and the quantum are two views of the same mirror, M in the spatial direction, m_Pl²/M in the inner one.

The meeting point of the ladder of steps and the mirror point of the loops also count the same loop: with s = −ln|q| = 2π·Im τ, the meeting point s = 1 lies at one radian, the mirror point s = 2π at the full turn. The threshold counts per radian, the mirror per turn.

# Part IV: The Levels

The same formulas hold on every level. Only mass and radius change. Every level receives its position from its neighbours: the end of the nucleus from the coupling α of the shell, the window of matter from the kind of bond of the atom, the spread of the star from what holds it, the burning stages from the iron of the nucleus, the smallest gas-bearing galaxy from the heat of its gas, the position of the galaxy relative to the threshold from the whole.

## 17 Particle, Coupling, Charge

An elementary particle is an oscillating state of the medium. Next to it, space remains practically flat. Gravity and electromagnetism have the same form, a **length of action divided by the quantum length**:

```
α_G = 3Δs_p/λ̄_p = Gm_p²/(ħc) = 5.906·10⁻³⁹          α = r_e/λ̄_e = 1/137.036
```

Two ways of imprinting space at a node must be distinguished. The **fold** is a magnitude: it changes the clock rate and has one sense of circulation, that of loss. The **winding** is a phase: it does not change the clock rate, is lossless and counts in both senses of circulation. Mass is fold, **charge is winding**: the phase of a node has a whole-number winding n around its circulation, the charge is Q_el = n·e with e the elementary charge, and the sign is the handedness. On the torus, n is a topological number (the first Chern number). It changes only when the amplitude passes through zero. This is why Coulomb's law remains 1/r² in every field, and why there is no electromagnetic threshold effect: the argument of the rule of return is the clock-rate gradient, never an electric force. In the state space the Lorentz force acts tangentially: on a moving charge its magnetic part does no work, it conserves energy and entropy and produces circulations and phases. Joule heat J·E is the channel into the transverse role. By Onsager and Casimir the transport splits into a symmetric part (conductivity, heat conduction, Seebeck, Peltier: transverse) and an antisymmetric one (Hall, Nernst: tangential). The magnetic field decides which share circulates and which is lost.

**Twist and three rates.** Fold and winding sit at the same node. The twist a_J = J/(Mc) is an imaginary displacement of this place. Both multipole series are M·(i·a_J)^l and Q_el·(i·a_J)^l (i the imaginary unit, l the order), hence J = Mc·a_J and μ = Q_el·c·a_J: from this follows, for every mass, charge and rotation, the same ratio of magnetic moment to angular momentum, g = 2. For the electron this is the Bohr magneton μ_B = eħ/(2m_e). Every mass has three rates: the fold rate c/(3Δs), the Compton rate c/λ̄ and the folding rate α_G·c/λ̄. The Compton rate is their geometric mean, once more a mirror. Below the Planck mass the fold rate is the fastest, above it the reverse, at the Planck mass all three are equal.

**Return of the winding.** In the field B the twist turns at the rate eB/M, for g = 2 exactly as fast as the orbit goes round, the orbit as a whole only half as fast. The difference the measurement reads is made by the return of the winding onto itself: counted per radian, with the weight α, once back, μ/μ_B = 1 + α/(2π) = 1.001161, measured 1.001160 (CODATA 2018). Independently, the hydrogen line, winding times twist at the place of the nucleus, reads the first return as 0.88·α/2π: without return 1418.95 MHz, with the first 1420.60 MHz, measured 1420.41 MHz. When the rate eB/M reaches the Compton rate, the field lies at the meeting point of the winding, B = M²c²/(eħ) = 4.41·10⁹ T (Schwinger field): there pairs arise from the medium.

**Matter in the field.** The three roles give three responses. Tangentially every orbit turns against the field without loss, the diamagnetism of every substance (hydrogen atom χ = −2.38·10⁻⁶ cm³/mol). Open windings align against the loss kT, paramagnetism, with two senses of circulation in the Fermi form m = tanh(μB/kT), small the Curie law. If the windings of neighbouring atoms lock in the same sense, the field of the others is a feedback with q = T_C/T: χ = (C/T)·Σqⁿ = C/(T − T_C), the tipping point q = 1 is the Curie temperature, ferromagnetism. Locking in opposite senses is q < 0, the antiferromagnet. Fully locked is the superconductor: two locked windings obey ψ̇ = Δω − K·sin ψ, the equation of the locking of two cycles (Josephson), the phase of the pair closes per circuit with Φ₀ = h/(2e) = 2.068·10⁻¹⁵ Wb, and the transverse channel is closed: no loss, no resistance, the field penetrates only to the closing length λ_L (Meissner).

## 18 The Nucleus: a Held Funnel

A **held funnel** is a body whose building blocks are held at the distance of a **holding length** λ_H. Its envelope is then R = λ_H·N^{1/3} for N building blocks. The atomic nucleus is such a funnel, held by its nucleons themselves.

**Derivation of the holding length from nuclear masses.** Through the mutual repulsion of its protons, the binding of a nucleus with Z protons and mass number A loses the Coulomb energy a_C·Z(Z − 1)/A^{1/3} with a_C = (3/5)·αħc/λ_H. Fitting the liquid-drop formula to all nuclei of AME2020 gives λ_H = 1.25 fm. Independently of that, **mirror nuclei**, in which Z and N are exchanged, differ only in winding. Their difference in binding is pure Coulomb energy and reads the holder as λ_H = 1.33 fm (36 pairs, AME2020).

The nucleons move inside the nucleus with v/c ≈ λ̄_p/λ_H = 0.17. Half the spread of the binding of ⁶²Ni independently gives 0.14. Size and binding read the same speed up to a factor of order one. Nuclear transitions run by themselves towards the smallest fold per nucleon, at ⁵⁶Fe. The largest binding per nucleon belongs to ⁶²Ni (AME2020).

**The tipping point.** The fissility x = E_C/(2E_S) compares the Coulomb energy E_C of the protons (winding) with the surface energy E_S of the holding. It is the gain of a feedback that tips over at x = 1: the restoring force of a deformation is ∝ 1 − x. The binding per nucleon is largest at x = ¼ (fitted to the measured nuclei of AME2020: x = 0.25). The tipping point is the meeting point of the nucleus. The heaviest known nuclei lie there (Z = 118, A = 294: x = 0.98), while the innermost electron of the same atoms is only at Zα = 0.86: matter ends at the meeting point of the nucleus before the shell reaches its own. Because x grows with α, the coupling of the shell sets the end of the nucleus.

**The valley.** The heaviest known nuclei are poor in neutrons. In the valley of stability, where at fixed A the fold energy Z·E_H + N·E_n − B is smallest (E_H, E_n the rest energies of hydrogen atom and neutron, N the number of neutrons, B the binding) (for each A the continuous Z of smallest fold energy meets the lightest measured isobar to within 0.32 protons, 219 mass numbers up to A = 238), the tipping point by the liquid-drop formula lies only at Z = 134 (A = 370), with the shell term of the model's closure numbers at Z = 181 to 191. The formula with shell term fits the data better, the scatter of the binding falls from 3.39 to 1.72 MeV. The tipping point itself is not measured, masses only up to Z = 110. The shell holds up to Z = 170: Zα = 1 (Z = 137) holds for a point-like nucleus. Around the nucleus with R = λ_H·A^{1/3} the binding of the innermost electron reaches twice the fold energy of the electron, 6c⁴·Δs_e/G, only there (Dirac equation).

**The shells.** The shell term follows from the funnel itself. The levels of the held funnel with twist (κ = 0.12, μ = 0.03) close at 2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82 and bind against their averaged filling with the shell spacing ħω = (5/6)·(3/2)^{4/3}·ħ²/(m_N·λ_H²)·A^{−1/3} = 38.0 MeV·A^{−1/3}. AME2020 reads independently 39.3, hence λ_H = 1.23 fm. With no free number in the shell term the binding scatters by 1.83 MeV, the spherical nuclei lack the closure at 126. At the ground-state spins (NUBASE2020, 850 odd nuclei with unambiguously measured spin) the filling meets 75 % of the parities and 38 % of the spins, outside the deformed regions 86 % and 49 %: the parity carries, the spin half of it.

## 19 The Atom, Closing and Opening

An atom is a small, fast-circulating vortex: tiny fold, large steepness. The shell of hydrogen circulates with v/c = α on the Bohr radius a_B = 0.529·10⁻¹⁰ m, at 6.58·10¹⁵ Hz. The atom too has its three radii: the threshold radius of the proton lies at r* = 32 fm, between nucleus and shell (a_B = 1631·r*). As a compact funnel, its interior remains Newtonian. The binding of the atom comes from the geometry of its shell, not from the depth of its fold.

**Closing forms.** The pure 1/r² slope closes every orbit, and orbits of equal energy bind equally, whatever their shape. This has a reason in form: the momenta of a Kepler orbit lie on a circle, and mapped onto the sphere S³ every orbit is a great circle. In the space of motions, not in position space, the funnel is a closed sphere. The n² states of a shell (n its number) are the vibrations of this sphere, 2n² with both senses of circulation. Only two slopes close every orbit (Bertrand's theorem): the 1/r² slope outside a funnel and the linear one inside a uniformly held body. Their shells close after

```
funnel (1/r²):          2, 10, 28, 60             2n² per shell
held body (r¹):         2, 8, 20, 40, 70, 112     O(k) = (k + 1)(k + 2)(k + 3)/3
```

**Opening couplings.** Every deviation from the pure form opens the closure, on every level: in the solar system the fold (3δ per revolution), in the galaxy the rule and the extended mass (rosettes, in the deep regime 254.6° lie between the pericentres), in the atom the inner shells that spread out the centre, in the nucleus the twist. Shell and nucleus open in mirrored fashion:

- In the shell, the orbits with the **smallest** orbital winding l bind more strongly, because they dive deep. Winding here means the number of nodes of the oscillation around the centre, not the winding of the charge (Section 17). The shells fill by n + l and close after 2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86 instead of after 28 and 60.
- In the nucleus, the twist pulls down, in every shell, the state with the **largest** orbital winding, j = l + ½ (j here the angular momentum of the nucleon, not the spin number), from the next shell. The nucleus then closes after O(k) + 2(k + 2), with k the number of the shell, that is after 28, 50, 82, 126 (k = 2 to 5). The next closure would be 184.

The orbit in a galaxy has a torus too. Its modulus τ_M carries in its real part the ratio of radial to azimuthal frequency, in its imaginary part the binding. For the point mass (Re τ_M)² = 1 + χ: χ = 0 gives the closed Kepler orbits, χ = 1 rosettes. The rule lifts the Kepler degeneracy κ = Ω (Ω the frequency of the orbit, κ that of the radial oscillation about the circular orbit) and replaces it by local resonances at rational κ/Ω.

**In the data.** The six strongest drops of the ionisation energy lie after Z = 2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86 (factor 4.6 at He down to 2.8 at Rn, PubChem), the six strongest jumps of the two-nucleon binding at N = 16, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126 and Z = 14, 20, 28, 38, 50, 82 (AME2020). The series meets 20, 28, 50, 82 and 126. The jumps at N = 16 and Z = 14, 38 lie at subshells within the series, and 8 does not show in the two-nucleon binding. The closure numbers of both levels thus follow from one closing form each and one coupling that opens it.

**The ladder of periods.** With the noble gas Og the seventh period closes at Z = 118 (PubChem). The periods are 2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, 32 long. The length of period p is the number of states with n + l = p + 1, that is 2k² with k the rounded-up half of p + 1: every step 2k² of the 1/r² form comes twice, because n + l = 2k − 1 and 2k have the same largest winding l = k − 1. The ladder is itself a closing form: a field that is mirrored in log r about a radius R (r ↔ R²/r) binds at the threshold E = 0 exactly at the couplings N(N + 1) with N = n + l, each step with 2k² states, k the rounded-up half of N. Its states at the threshold are spherical harmonics, u = √r·P_N^{2l+1}((r − R)/(r + R)) with P the associated Legendre function: the step n + l is the degree on a sphere whose poles are nucleus and infinity and whose equator is R. The winding l gives the order 2l + 1, and the mirror r ↔ R²/r is the reflection at the equator. In log r this field is v·f(1 − f) with f = 1/(eˣ + 1), x = ln(r/R): the same function as the rule, only in the other counting, the winding in both senses of circulation, f(x) + f(−x) = 1, instead of the magnitude in one, ν(s) + ν(−s) = 1. The shell is a funnel held by the counting rule of the electrons, like the white dwarf, with R ∝ Z^{−1/3} (Thomas–Fermi): its field follows the mirror form inside to 0.6 % and opens outside. Its height v = 3.44·Z^{2/3} lies from Ne to Og in the window N(N + 1) < v < (N + 1)(N + 2) with N = p + 1, the field binds exactly the occupied steps. 99 of the 118 elements fill exactly by n + l. The 19 deviations lie in d and f shells within the periods and leave the closure numbers untouched: eight of them close a half or full shell (d⁵, d¹⁰, f⁷), seven begin the f shell with a d electron, the smaller winding, four give an s electron to the d shell.

**Beyond 118.** The eighth period would have 50 places, with 8s (Z = 119, 120) and the first g shell (l = 4), and would close at 168. There the height of the held funnel lies in the window (104.8 between 90 and 110). Held in the valley by both formulas are the elements up to Z = 133, only close to the valley (N ≈ 202–235). By the formula with shell term they reach the limit of the shell, Z = 170. Alpha decay is a passage through the Coulomb threshold, lg T_α = a·Z/√Q_α + b·Z + c with T_α the half-life, Q_α the energy of the decay and three fitted numbers a, b, c. On 82 even-even alpha emitters (NUBASE2020, AME2020) this fits to 0.51 decades, and in the held range the nuclei in the valley would hold against alpha decay for seconds to hours.

## 20 Matter: the Coupling Window

Two coupled cycles with phase difference ψ obey ψ̇ = Δω − 2k·cos φ·sin ψ, with Δω the difference of their eigenfrequencies, the coupling k (here not the Boltzmann constant) and its phase φ. They lock in when |Δω| ≤ 2k|cos φ|, and their torus then contracts onto a circle. Only the lossy part k·cos φ of the coupling locks, the rest merely shifts the frequencies. Too weak a coupling lets the cycles drift apart. In general, for cycles θ and slow structure Z: θ̇ = ω(Z) + K(θ, Z), Ż = G(θ, Z) − D(Z)·Z, with eigenfrequencies ω, cross-coupling K, feed G and loss D. A common medium with pair coupling k carries at most N ≈ 1 + 2W/(πk) free cycles in a frequency band W. Above that they lock in. If a feedback grows to q = 1, Σqⁿ diverges: before this tipping point 1 − q ∝ δ^{1/2} (δ here the distance to the tipping point), the hysteresis window opens with the distance to the power 3/2, and the return rate falls with its square root (critical slowing down). From T² to T¹ lead three ways: locking (both cycles go on oscillating, bound), kinking, and the loss of a cycle. The number of independent cycles is the number of neutral Lyapunov exponents, transverse to the torus they are negative. Stability is motion without an obligation to repeat: the feedback must take hold before a perturbation builds up. Bifurcations are changes of role: the birth of a cycle makes a transverse direction tangential, locking makes a tangential one transverse. Hysteresis needs no store, the state is only the sequence of the extremes not yet erased. Noise and loss come from the same damping. In between lies the **coupling window** k_min < k < k_lock. A substance is the coupling window of its atoms: solid is locked (the lattice as identification), liquid is inside the window, gaseous is frayed. Melting gives up the identification of the lattice, not the cohesion.

The width of the window depends on the level below: across 93 elements, T_boil/T_melt ranges from 0.81 (As, which sublimes, so the window is closed) to 8.2 (Ga), and the more firmly the atom binds, the narrower the window (rank correlation with the ionisation energy −0.48, PubChem). The trend runs between kinds of bond (noble gases and halogens narrow, metals wide), not within a group.

## 21 Stars

**Mass scale.** The fold of a star is fixed by the fold and the quantum length of the proton:

```
M_* = m_Pl³/m_p² = 1.853 M☉,        λ̄_p² = ℓ_P·3Δs_*
```

The star is the mirror image of the Planck point at the quantum length of the proton: its fold stands to the quantum length of the proton as the latter stands to the Planck length.

The state part on their binding. In SPARC (Υ_d = 0.67), below v_flat = 40 km/s 72 % of the baryons are gas, above 150 km/s 12 % (median): the small, gas-rich regions carry the state part. What is counted is the accessible state space, not the place: per baryon the largest region counts, a sub-binding within it adds no measure. Hence only regions that are themselves the largest can lose, single galaxies, not galaxies in groups.

**Size and lower limit.** In the deep regime the inactive measure of a region is (R_ta/r*)^{3/2}, with the turnaround radius R_ta ∝ M^{1/3} and r* ∝ M^{1/2}, per baryon ∝ M^{−1/4}: small and late-bound regions carry more. The lower limit is set by the gas itself: only what thermal motion holds becomes bound, in the deep regime M_Gas = (81/4)·σ⁴/(Ga₀), independent of density. With the gas pressure of the slow dwarfs (SPARC, 8–10 km/s), M_Gas = 0.6–1.4·10⁷ M☉. Written without mass, a region holds only gas with σ < (4/81)^{1/4}·v_flat = 0.47·v_flat. With 8–10 km/s there is no gas-bearing disc below v_flat = 17–21 km/s. In SPARC the smallest measured rotation is 17.8 km/s, the smallest flat one 18.6 km/s.

The whole is thus not an equilibrium but a **feedback loop**:

```
fold (fixed)  →  restoring  →  stretching of the loops
stretching  ⊣  binding                     (stretching keeps regions open)
binding  →  inactive escape directions  →  return  →  stretching
```

More stretching keeps regions open and so lowers the state part: the loop limits itself. Its lag is the time until the turnaround of a region, a time in space, not τ_D. Q and the vacuum part are form (nome of the cell, Λ), fixed in time. The escape measure is state and follows the bound measure of the largest region in each case. An amount "today" is a phase of the loop, not a property of space. The measurement reads form and state as one fixed dark energy: Ω_DE presupposes a fixed dark energy and ΣΩ = 1, w₀ and w_a a smooth fluid, Ω_c reads the mode ξ as dust, and along the light cone the observer sees earlier phases of the same loop. The loop saturates where dark energy catches up with matter. The vacuum part alone would catch up with it only at z = −0.10, three parts do so at z = 0.30. The small state part hardly shifts this, the moment hangs on a₀ alone. After that, the backflow dominates: gas is heated above the binding of its region and releases its inactive measure, regions merge, gas turns into stars. Dark energy has a maximum and weakens afterwards. This is the direction the distances weakly show.

**Two paths of backflow.** *Merging:* when a region falls into a larger one, its escape directions are already bound in the parent field and cease to be escape directions. The number of turnarounds remains. Whether this raises or lowers the state part depends on the slope n of the spectrum: typically, regions M_z ∝ (1 + z)^{−6/(n+3)} bind at time z, so R_ta/r* ∝ (1 + z)^{−(n+2)/(n+3)}. Below n = −2 merging lowers the state part, above it raises it. The boundary lies at 3–4·10¹² M☉, set by the equality of radiation and fold at z_eq = 3402. *Heat:* when gas is heated above the binding of its region, it leaves the seam and its inactive measure is released. Gas at 100–300 km/s is held only by regions from 1.4·10¹¹ to 1.2·10¹³ M☉. If gas ΔN leaves the seam, the inactive measure drops by ΔN·(ln B − ¾), with B the coefficient of the region (the author's derivation). When the vacuum part halts the growth of structure, new binding ends but heating does not: then the backflow dominates.

## 27 Cycle, Memory and Entropy

On the largest scale the **cycle** closes: at the bottleneck the medium condenses into fold again. The total entropy grows in every round, and the cosmos runs a spiral, not into a heat death. The cycle closes in form, not in state.

**Memory.** Folded space does not forget: every vortex leaves a lasting crease, and new systems use the existing grooves. The crease is form, not a store beside space. In the cosmos it is a state of the closure rule: what lasts is its weight w, which depends on the smallest expansion rate in the history. A region once bound stays bound. Memory is the number of turnarounds of a region: its phase runs only forward under a restoring slope, the weight drops as a step at the first turnaround. What is protected is the count, not the single loop.

**Entropy and heat.** The bottleneck is, at the place, the fold r ≤ 6Δs and, in the whole, the horizon. On both levels the same form of entropy holds, S = 36πk·(Δs/ℓ_P)² = 4πk·(M/m_Pl)², for the Sun 1.05·10⁷⁷ k. The temperature of the fold is kT = ħc/(24πΔs), that of the horizon twice as high for the same fold. Heat is here a loop length: the thermal length ħc/kT of every closure is its loop, for the fold 24πΔs = 4π·r₀, for the horizon 2πc/H, for the inner direction ℓ₀ = 2Λ (kT_I = ħa₀/c). The entropy is the coupling, S = 4πk·(M/m_Pl)². For the horizon T = ħc/(12π·k·Δs_H) with Δs_H = c/(6H). Order grows inside only as long as more entropy is exported than produced. This is syntropy, Syn = S_eq − S, with S_eq the entropy in equilibrium. It changes as dSyn/dt = J − Ṡ_i, with the entropy export J and the production Ṡ_i ≥ 0. At the bottleneck of the whole the medium condenses into fold again. Λ grows and the vacuum part falls. The entropy belonging to the vacuum part depends, like that of a fold, only on the square of a fold length, S_Λ = (45/π³)·k·(Λ/ℓ_P)², and requires Λ̇/Λ ≥ Ṡ_i/(2S_Λ). This minimum rate is tiny and does not act on the dark energy today. For gravity, compression increases entropy: the fold is, in the small, the place of greatest entropy and, in the large, of the simplest form.

## 28 The Web

The torus has Euler characteristic zero. Therefore every form of the slope obeys

```
funnels − filament saddles + wall saddles − voids = 0
```

The closed medium enforces the web of nodes, filaments, walls and voids. Between two funnels there is always a filament saddle, between two voids a wall saddle. Fewer than 1, 3, 3, 1 is impossible, the Betti numbers of the torus: the simplest form, cos x + cos y + cos z, has exactly one funnel, three filaments, three walls and one void. The three filaments of the smallest web are the three loops that carry the dark energy. Reversing the slope exchanges funnel with void and filament with wall: the web is a mirror that only the fold breaks, because only the funnels carry fold.

## 29 The Observer's Fallacy

When the rule of the whole changes, a measured value at a place deviates, and the observer invents a local thing as its carrier. In the model nothing behaves differently. The rule of the medium has changed. For every quantity one must therefore ask in which direction of view it holds: **at the place** (L, in space), **in the inner direction** (I, orthogonal to space: return, Λ, ϖ) or **for the whole** (G, the torus as a cell). And in which state: breathing along or bound, in which epoch, for which observer.

| Phenomenon | read as a thing | as a state of the medium |
|---|---|---|
| mass | substance in space | fold depth Δs |
| gravity | emitted force | slope g |
| time | a flow of its own | difference in unrolling δ |
| expansion | bodies fly apart | the loops stretch |
| dark matter | invisible particle | changed rule of motion below the threshold a₀ |

**Rule instead of source.** The difference between an additional substance and a changed rule can be stated precisely. Closure is an **operator** 𝒫 acting on the generator of motion, L → 𝒫L, with 𝒫_q = (1 − q)·Σ qᵐTᵐ, T the step by one return (one turn in the inner direction) and qᵐ its weight after m returns. It deletes no degrees of freedom and preserves the measure, while certain directions become inactive and pass into the return. In closed form 𝒫_q = (1 − q)(I − qT)⁻¹: for q → 0, 𝒫 = I, open and Newtonian. For q → 1, space is closed. On the torus, applied to a power kernel, 𝒫 is the lattice sum after Eisenstein. Its lowest series over the return steps of one direction is Σqⁿ = q/(1 − q), the addition of the circular orbit. The bound measure grows only as long as a parameter wanders. A source, by contrast, adds density:

| | Source (dark matter as substance) | Operator 𝒫 |
|---|---|---|
| where it acts | ρ → ρ + ρ_DM | L → 𝒫L |
| potential | additive | multiplicative |
| localisable | yes | no |
| degrees of freedom | additional | existing ones become bound |
| time structure | without memory | memory (weight w, handover of the mode) |

The density read off, ρ_X = ∇·(a − a_N)/(4πG), is an output of the calculation and not even unique: different distributions produce the same external field. If all tests run alike, source and operator are just two descriptions. If one test runs robustly apart, they can be told apart.

**Entanglement.** Two entangled particles may lie arbitrarily far apart in space. In the inner direction they share the same node. No signal travels. Both measurements act on the same node. Their correlation is the fundamental vibration of the winding, E(Δ) = −cos Δ with Δ the angle between the two measurement directions, and gives S = 2√2 (S here Bell's measure of correlation, not the entropy). As a property given along, S ≤ 2 would remain.

## 30 What Can Be Tested

- Dark energy weakens late (w₀ > −1, w_a < 0).
- The next closure number of nuclei is 184.

- The vortex of the rule can be tested in discs by ∂_z a_R ≠ ∂_R a_z.

This tests the equation, not gravity: in the laboratory s ≈ 3·10⁵, there is no laboratory prediction that only the model makes.

## 31 What Is Open

- **The reading of Λ** as the entire fold of the cell is posited, supported by the mirror between part and whole.
- **Q** is, counted per radian, 2π − 1 (the author's counting, the derivation is still outstanding), 1.3 σ from the measurement. Whether counting per radian is compelling remains open.
- **α and m_p/m_e** are content, not form. They follow from no equation of the model.
- **The state part:** the data read its course, open is its size, the share of bound gas.
- **H₀ of the Cepheid ladder** lies above the value of the breathing at every z. Whether its calibration is to be read differently in the model will only be shown by the Cepheid data themselves.
- **The warp of the Milky Way:** does not fit yet.
- **Shear:** in KiDS-1000 the model and ΛCDM lie equal, both demand a lower amplitude. Whether the mode handed over in galaxies lowers the growth below the turnaround scale is open.
- **Ultra-diffuse galaxies:** six gas-rich ones rotate like Newton alone, the rule demands two and a half times the velocity. Their outer point lies at the seam, where the local rule and the measure of the whole give the same, so a mode that was not handed over does not carry them either. Gas-rich isolated dwarfs at the same place follow the rule, their dispersion needs no inclination. Their inclination is small and read only from the shape of the gas.
- **Red lenses:** around isolated galaxies that no longer form stars the lensing lies above the rule of their stars. Whether the gas of their past carries it as for the dwarfs, only their metallicity will show.
- **Elements beyond 118:** the tipping point of the nucleus in the valley of stability (the neutron number per proton number with the largest binding) is computed, not measured (Section 18). Open are the filling of the g shell and the lifetime against fission.
